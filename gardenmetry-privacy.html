Gardenmetry Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Developer: Magnuslabs

Gardenmetry is a garden planner, harvest log and yield-analysis app. It is built so that your garden records stay with you. This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and no sign-up is needed.
  • Your harvests, plants, beds, care notes and photos are stored only on your phone.
  • We do not run servers that receive your garden data, and we never sell or share it.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your phone and is not uploaded anywhere by us. This includes harvests, plantings, beds and layouts, care entries, your garden name and location, and settings. If you uninstall the app or use More → Erase everything, this data is removed from the phone.

Photos and camera

If you choose to attach a photo, the app asks for permission to use the camera or your photo library. Chosen photos are resized and saved inside the app on your phone. They are not uploaded. You can decline these permissions and still use every other feature.

Location and weather

Gardenmetry does not use your phone's GPS. If you type in your garden's address or town, the app looks up its coordinates and local weather using these free public services:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for place search, forecasts and historical weather used to estimate frost dates;
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) as a backup for address search.

Only the text you search for and the garden's coordinates are sent, over an encrypted connection, to get those results. No name, account or device identifier is sent. These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. Their servers may briefly log standard request information such as your IP address. If you skip the location step, no location is ever sent.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone for plantings and care that are due. They are created on the device, and nothing is sent to us.

Backups you export

When you use Export backup, the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes, such as email, a cloud drive or another app, is your choice and is governed by that service. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick.

Children

Gardenmetry is a general gardening tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
